# Recent high-engagement videos (same topics as the Groww day-trade brief)

**Niche used:** Groww / India day trading — equity MIS, F&O risk, fees, capital for a small daily rupee target, SEBI retail-loss data, VWAP / opening-range setups.  
**Window:** mostly **last 1–8 weeks** (Aug 2026); a few **last 3–6 months** kept only when they match the brief unusually well.  
**Pulled:** 30 Aug 2026. View/like counts move fast; treat them as a snapshot.

Engagement here = **views in a short time** plus **like rate** (likes ÷ views). News clips often have high views and weaker likes; how-to videos often have the opposite.

## What is currently winning

1. **SEBI FY26 F&O loss report** (this week / last 10 days) — “88% / 9 out of 10 still lose, 45–46 lakh left, fees ate the rest.”
2. **Groww + SEBI CAS / session close rules** — official + explainer videos on how closing rules hit intraday & F&O.
3. **App how-tos on Groww** — “intraday kaise kare” still prints views in Hindi/Telugu if the demo is clear.
4. **Fee / broker comparison 2026** — Groww vs Zerodha vs Angel vs Dhan.
5. **Honest ₹500 / small-capital F&O reality checks** — not “guaranteed income.”
6. **One named setup** — VWAP, 5:30 pm method, Nifty ORB — one playbook, not a 40-indicator dump.
7. **Daily Nifty/Bank Nifty levels** — high volume, weaker evergreen value; good for *format*, not for copying calls.

## Gaps you can still own

Almost nobody in the last month is combining **all** of:

1. Groww-specific **all-in fee / break-even** on a small ticket  
2. **Position size** for a ₹200–500 *aspirational* day with a 1–2% risk cap  
3. **SEBI FY26** “costs turned winners into losers”  
4. “This is **not** a daily salary”

That combo is closer to the research doc than “Nifty tomorrow 24,300.”

Avoid promising profits. The videos that travel right now are **loss/cost explainers** and **one-setup tutorials**, not “₹500 guaranteed.”
